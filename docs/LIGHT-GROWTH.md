# Light and growth: sources

Filled 28 November 2026 by the default method (CLAUDE.md): a value for each
species written from knowledge first, then checked against NC State's Plant
Toolbox and the Morton Arboretum, which both give light and growth rate in
labeled fields, and revised.

## How disagreements were settled

- **Light.** NC State and Morton list every light level a plant grows in, so
  they often say sun to part shade for trees that do best in full sun. Where
  the Forest Service's Silvics or FEIS classes a tree as intolerant of shade,
  it is `full sun` (bitternut and mockernut hickory, honeylocust, sweetgum,
  sassafras, black locust, the aspens, black willow, several oaks and pines).
  Otherwise the sources' range stands, even where it differed from the first
  draft (tree-of-heaven, for one, is moderately shade tolerant).
- **Growth.** Where the sources and the draft differ by one step, the value
  spans both (`slow to medium`, `medium to fast`). Where they differ by two
  steps and nothing settles it, growth is left blank (devil's walkingstick,
  bitternut hickory).
- **Cultivars** take their species' values. Growth is left blank on the four
  much smaller than their species (Amber Glow dawn redwood, Snow Fountains
  cherry, Emerald arborvitae, Camperdown elm).
