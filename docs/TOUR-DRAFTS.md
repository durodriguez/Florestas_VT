# Tour drafts

Walks drafted from the data and **not yet on the map**. A draft becomes a tour
when someone who knows the trees has walked it, checked each stop is the tree
described and worth the stop, and rewritten the notes; then its rows go into
`data/tours.csv` and `data/tour-stops.csv` (DATA-MODEL.md, "tours.csv and
tour-stops.csv").

## Native or not (drafted November 2026)

Six stops on Central Campus, each a non-native tree with a Vermont native of
the same genus beside it, so the two can be compared side by side. About 850 m
from stop to stop, north to south.

Picked by script: every non-native tree (taxa.csv `origin` introduced or
vermont-invasive) with a native of its genus within 35 m, then one pair per
comparison, chosen for the shortest walk. Freeman maple was left out: it is a
cross of two natives, not a foreign tree.

| Stop | Non-native | Native | Apart | What to compare (to check on the spot) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Norway maple, UVM-3232 | Sugar maple, UVM-4181 | 4 m | Snap a leaf stalk: Norway maple's sap is milky, sugar maple's clear. Norway maple is marked invasive in Vermont. |
| 2 | Scotch pine, UVM-0594 | Eastern white pine, UVM-0588 | 29 m | Count the needles in a bundle: two, short and twisted, against white pine's five, long and soft. Scotch pine's upper bark is orange. |
| 3 | Norway spruce, UVM-0745 | White spruce, UVM-4005 | 6 m | Norway spruce's side branches hang like curtains and its cones are long; white spruce's cones are short and its crushed needles smell sharp. |
| 4 | Lombardy poplar, UVM-0781 | Bigtooth aspen, UVM-4011 | 5 m | A narrow column of a clone, every one a cutting from one male tree, against a native aspen with big, coarse teeth on its leaves. |
| 5 | Kousa dogwood, UVM-4728 | Flowering dogwood, UVM-4726 | 16 m | Both young (under 3 in across). Kousa blooms weeks later, with pointed bracts and raspberry-like fruit; flowering dogwood's bracts are notched. |
| 6 | Colorado blue spruce, UVM-3802 | White spruce, UVM-4185 | 5 m | Blue-gray, stiff, sharp needles against white spruce's softer green: a western tree beside a northern one. |

**Before it goes on the map:** the "what to compare" notes are standard
identification marks, written here, not checked on these trees. Confirm each
stop, especially that the dogwoods are big enough to be worth one, and that
stops 3 and 6 (two spruce comparisons) are not one too many. Athletic Campus
has a pair the Central walk lacks, English oak UVM-1672 two metres from white
oak UVM-1673, if a second walk is wanted.
